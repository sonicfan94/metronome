Frequently asked

I can't hear the metronome.
Check the click-volume slider in the metronome card isn't at zero, and turn your ringer/media volume up. SteadyState is designed to play even when the silent switch is on.
The click stopped after a phone call or alarm.
Return to the app — it resumes automatically. If it doesn't, tap STOP then PLAY.
Does it work offline?
Yes. SteadyState is fully offline and needs no internet connection or account.
Where is my data stored?
Entirely on your device. Nothing is collected or transmitted. See the privacy policy.
The screen keeps dimming while I practice.
SteadyState keeps the screen awake only while the metronome is playing. Press PLAY and it will stay on.
