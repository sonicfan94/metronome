SteadyState — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 June 2026

SteadyState does not collect any personal data. Everything the app stores stays on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. SteadyState has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking. The app does not require an internet connection and works fully offline.

What stays on your device

Your tempo, time signature, click volume, haptics preference, and any practice presets you save are stored locally on your device so the app can remember them between sessions. This information never leaves your device and is never transmitted to us or anyone else. Deleting the app removes it.

Data sharing

We do not share, sell, or transfer any information, because we do not collect any.

Children's privacy

SteadyState is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date above.
