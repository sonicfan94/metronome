## Description
SteadyState is a focused practice companion for musicians — a rock-solid metronome
paired with the reference tools you actually reach for while practicing.

**A metronome you can trust**
• Web Audio scheduling for steady, low-jitter timing from 40 to 240 BPM
• Accent the downbeat, set any beats-per-bar, and switch subdivisions (eighths,
  triplets, sixteenths)
• Tap tempo, adjustable click volume, and optional haptic pulse on every beat
• Plays through the silent switch and mixes with your backing tracks
• Keeps the screen awake while you play, recovers cleanly after calls and Siri

**A built-in speed trainer**
Ramp the tempo up automatically as you practice — set the step, the interval (bars
or seconds), and a target tempo, then just play.

**Practice references for four instruments**
Browse scales and patterns with on-screen tab and fretboard diagrams for guitar and
bass, keyboard diagrams with fingering for piano, and groove grids plus rudiment
sticking for drums.

**Yours, and only yours**
Save named presets for your routines. Everything runs fully offline and stays on
your device — no account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
